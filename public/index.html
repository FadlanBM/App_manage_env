Client Integration Guide

How your client apps (Flutter, Node.js, Python, Golang) fetch and inject decrypted secrets dynamically at runtime.

1
Send Credentials

Client calls GET /api/client/secrets passing X-App-Id and X-App-Secret in headers.

2
Server Verification & Decryption

Server verifies bcrypt hash of secret, fetches AES-256-GCM ciphertexts, and decrypts them in memory.

3
Inject to Process Environment

Client receives key-value pairs and injects them directly into process.env before booting application services.
